AI Engineer, LLM Applications

Join a small team building customer-facing assistants on top of large language models. You will own the quality of our retrieval, prompting and evaluation pipelines.

Responsibilities
- Design retrieval-augmented generation systems and measure their quality
- Build offline and online evaluation, including regression tests for prompts and models
- Reduce hallucinations and handle failure modes safely
- Work with product managers to define what good looks like

Requirements
- Experience shipping LLM-powered features to production
- Solid Python and API development skills
- Ability to design experiments and interpret results
- Good judgement about safety, privacy and when not to automate
