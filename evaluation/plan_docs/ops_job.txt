Logistics Operations Manager

You will run day-to-day operations for a regional distribution hub, balancing service levels, cost and safety for a team of about 100 people.

Responsibilities
- Plan capacity and staffing against seasonal demand
- Manage carrier relationships and service performance
- Maintain a strong safety culture and meet compliance requirements
- Coach supervisors and handle difficult people situations fairly

Requirements
- Several years of operations leadership in warehousing or distribution
- Experience managing performance metrics and continuous improvement
- Excellent communication and problem solving under pressure
